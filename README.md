Coral Heat is an original top-down open-world crime game that plays in a desktop browser from static files: explore the sun-soaked city of Coral Bay on foot and by car, steal and drive any car, outrun a five-star wanted system and its police chases, fight, work through a five-mission story for fixer Marisol Vega, run taxi fares, buy gear, respray the heat away, and save your progress — with traffic, pedestrians, a HUD and minimap, day and night, and synthesized sound throughout.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Play with WASD and the mouse; F enters a car, Space is the handbrake, Esc pauses.
